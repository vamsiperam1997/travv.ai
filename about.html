About Travv™ AI Inc.

Colorado Springs, CO · Founded 2026 · travv.ai

Travv™ AI Inc. is a digital-native travel technology startup. We build an AI-powered mobile navigation app that helps users discover attractions, plan routes, and book travel — all in one place. Available globally on iOS and Android.

What We Do

Travv is a freemium mobile app that combines GPS navigation with AI-powered discovery of tourist attractions, restaurants, and hotels along your route. Users can also book flights, hotels, and car rentals directly in the app. Premium features are available via monthly and annual subscriptions.

  • Turn-by-turn GPS navigation with real-time traffic
  • AI-powered discovery of attractions and stops along your route
  • In-app flight, hotel, and car rental booking
  • AI voice assistant for hands-free trip planning
  • Freemium model with premium subscription upgrade
