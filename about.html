About Travv™ AI Inc.

Colorado Springs, CO · Founded 2026 · travv.ai

Travv™ AI Inc. is a digital-native travel technology startup building the future of intelligent navigation. Our mobile app — available on iOS and Android — combines turn-by-turn GPS navigation with AI-powered discovery of attractions, travel bookings, and voice-based itinerary planning.

Our Company

Travv™ AI Inc. is incorporated in the United States and headquartered in Colorado Springs, Colorado. We are a small, focused startup building a globally available, cloud-native product accessible to anyone with a smartphone.

Our product — the Travv app — is live on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Users can download and use Travv for free, with premium features available via subscription.

Our Product

The Travv app is a full-stack travel companion. Here is what it does:

Navigation & Discovery

  • Turn-by-turn GPS navigation with real-time traffic and rerouting
  • Automatic discovery of tourist attractions, restaurants, parks, and hidden gems along your route
  • Save favorite stops and plan multi-destination trips
  • Offline-capable map support for areas with limited connectivity

AI-Powered Features

  • AI voice assistant (powered by Google Gemini) for hands-free trip planning
  • Intelligent recommendations based on your preferences and route history
  • Natural language search: "find me a coffee shop with good wifi near my route"
  • AI-generated itineraries for road trips and multi-day journeys

Travel Booking (In-App)

  • Search and book flights directly in the app via travel API partners (Amadeus, Duffel)
  • Hotel and motel search and reservations via Booking.com and partner APIs
  • Car rental search and booking
  • Booking management and confirmation all within Travv

Subscription & Monetization

  • Free tier with core navigation and basic discovery features
  • Premium subscription for unlimited route saves, AI features, and advanced booking tools
  • Subscriptions processed via Apple App Store and Google Play (RevenueCat)
  • Travel booking commissions from partner APIs

Technology & Infrastructure

Travv is built on a modern, cloud-native stack designed to scale globally:

  • Maps & Navigation: Google Maps Platform
  • AI / Recommendations: Google Gemini AI
  • Backend & Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL, real-time, authentication)
  • Subscription Management: RevenueCat
  • Travel Booking APIs: Amadeus, Duffel, Booking.com
  • Payments: Apple App Store & Google Play billing
  • Mobile: React Native (cross-platform iOS & Android)
  • Infrastructure: Cloud-hosted, scalable, globally available

Business Model

Travv operates a digital-native, mobile-first business model with multiple revenue streams:

  • Freemium App: Free download with premium upgrade — available globally via App Store and Google Play
  • Subscription Revenue: Monthly and annual subscription plans unlocking AI features, unlimited saves, and priority routing
  • Booking Commissions: Revenue share from flight, hotel, and car rental bookings completed via Travv
  • Future: AI-powered concierge services, enterprise/fleet navigation, and B2B travel planning tools
